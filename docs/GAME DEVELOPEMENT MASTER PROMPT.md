# GAME DEVELOPMENT MASTER PROMPT
# AI: WORLD DOMINION
### A Global Corporate Expansion & AI Evolution Simulator

Create a complete, playable, browser-based strategy simulation game inspired by the core gameplay loop and world-map presentation of Plague Inc., but with an original corporate domination theme.

The player starts as the CEO of a small artificial intelligence startup and gradually builds it into the most powerful AI corporation in the world.

The central gameplay experience is to develop AI, expand corporate infrastructure, influence the digital world, establish a physical global presence, generate revenue, increase company valuation, and ultimately achieve worldwide AI dominance.

The game must feature two interconnected global maps:

1. **INTERNET MAP** – Expand your AI's presence throughout the digital ecosystem.
2. **REAL WORLD MAP** – Expand your physical infrastructure and corporate influence across countries.

Both maps must be interactive, visually engaging, and mechanically connected.

The game must have meaningful progression, random events, strategic decisions, upgrades, financial management, consequences, and multiple endings.

---

# 1. ART DIRECTION AND VISUAL STYLE

Use Plague Inc. as a reference for map-based strategy presentation, not as an exact visual reproduction.

**Visual identity:**
- Low-poly minimalist 3D aesthetic.
- Simplified low-poly globe and geographical landmasses.
- Flat-shaded countries and regions.
- Clean, readable interfaces.
- Dark futuristic technology atmosphere.
- Minimalist icons and visual indicators.
- Subtle animated particles and connection lines.
- Smooth zooming and panning.
- Professional strategy-game dashboard.

**Color palette:**
- Background: Deep navy / almost black.
- Primary: Cyan and electric blue.
- Secondary: Purple and violet.
- Financial growth: Green.
- Warning: Orange.
- Critical threats: Red.
- Neutral countries: Muted gray.

**World map presentation:**

The map should be the main gameplay area, taking up most of the screen.

Countries must be individually selectable and visually distinguishable.

Use color overlays to communicate different information:

- Blue: Countries where your AI has digital influence.
- Purple: Countries where your corporation operates.
- Green: Countries with profitable operations.
- Orange: Countries with regulatory restrictions.
- Red: Countries experiencing major incidents.
- Gray: Countries not yet entered.

Use animated connection lines to represent AI network expansion, data-center connections, and corporate infrastructure.

Avoid excessive interface clutter. The map should remain the visual focus.

# 2. MAIN GAME CONCEPT

You are the founder and CEO of an AI startup.

You begin with:

- $10,000,000 starting capital.
- One small AI company.
- One basic AI model.
- A small amount of GPU computing power.
- Limited digital-world influence.
- No international offices.
- No data centers.
- No established investors.

Your initial objective is to develop your AI and establish your first customers.

As the game progresses, your company can become a multinational technology corporation.

Eventually, you can:
- Develop increasingly intelligent AI models.
- Generate billions in revenue.
- Build international data centers.
- Establish global branches.
- Acquire other technology companies.
- Integrate AI into businesses and public infrastructure.
- Influence the digital ecosystem.
- Become a dominant global technology provider.

The ultimate objective is to achieve one of the available endings.

# 3. TWO INTERCONNECTED WORLD MAPS

The player must be able to switch between two maps through a prominent map navigation control.

## MAP A: INTERNET WORLD

This represents your company's presence throughout the global digital ecosystem.

It is not simply a geographical map. It represents digital adoption, platform penetration, AI-generated content distribution, and network influence.

Display a world map with countries that can be selected.

Each country has its own digital statistics:

- Internet population.
- Digital adoption.
- AI adoption rate.
- Social media activity.
- Content consumption.
- Digital infrastructure.
- AI competition.
- Regulatory restrictions.
- Your company's digital penetration.

### Digital expansion mechanics

The player can evolve their AI and deploy different types of digital technologies.

**A. AI Content Generation**

Develop capabilities for generating:

- AI-generated articles.
- AI-generated images.
- AI-generated videos.
- AI-generated advertisements.
- AI-generated music.
- AI-generated documents.
- Automated social media content.
- Synthetic digital personalities.

Each capability increases your company's digital presence.

For gameplay purposes, content distribution is represented through abstract adoption and saturation statistics rather than actual platform manipulation.

**B. AI Automation Network**

Deploy AI agents that can perform simulated business activities:

- Customer support.
- Business automation.
- Content production.
- Research assistance.
- Data processing.
- Software development.
- Enterprise workflow automation.

These agents generate recurring revenue and increase adoption.

**C. Digital Saturation**

Introduce an AI content saturation mechanic.

Each country has a digital saturation percentage.

As your AI-generated content and services spread, digital saturation increases.

Higher saturation produces:
- Greater visibility.
- More users.
- Higher advertising revenue.
- Increased demand for AI services.
- More opportunities for enterprise contracts.

However, excessive AI-generated content creates negative consequences:

- Content fatigue.
- Reduced audience trust.
- Platform restrictions.
- AI content regulations.
- Increased competition.
- Reputation damage.

The player must balance expansion and digital reputation.

**D. AI Security and Vulnerability Research**

Develop cybersecurity capabilities as a technology evolution branch.

Research:
- AI security testing.
- Vulnerability detection.
- Automated threat analysis.
- Defensive AI systems.
- Enterprise security products.
- AI model protection.

Security incidents and vulnerabilities can occur randomly if the company neglects security.

All vulnerability and cyber-incident mechanics must remain abstract game statistics. Do not implement actual exploit instructions or real-world attack functionality.

**E. Synthetic Fraud and Misinformation Events**

The game world should simulate the consequences of malicious AI applications.

Examples:
- AI-generated scam incidents.
- Deepfake controversies.
- Synthetic identity scandals.
- AI-generated misinformation.
- Fraudulent AI applications.

These are primarily negative world events, not guaranteed profit-generating upgrades.

The player can choose to:
- Invest in detection systems.
- Improve content authenticity.
- Establish AI governance.
- Cooperate with platforms.
- Improve transparency.

Ignoring these events can damage reputation, reduce adoption, and trigger regulatory investigations.

Each country should contain attributes:

- Labor costs.
- Technology adoption.
- Government regulation.
- Corporate taxation.
- Political stability.

### Physical expansion mechanics

**A. Build Data Centers**

Allow the player to select a country and establish a data center.

Data centers provide:
- GPU computing capacity.
- AI processing power.
- Model training capacity.
- Inference capacity.
- Additional service capacity.
- Recurring operating costs.

Each data center must have:

- Construction cost.
- GPU capacity.
- Electricity consumption.
- Maintenance costs.
- Revenue contribution.
- Upgrade level.
- Operational status.

Data centers should be visually represented by small low-poly infrastructure icons on the world map.

**B. GPU Infrastructure**

GPU capacity is one of the most important resources in the game.

Allow players to:

- Purchase additional GPU capacity.
- Upgrade existing computing clusters.
- Research advanced computing hardware.
- Improve energy efficiency.
- Expand server infrastructure.
- Build regional computing networks.

Higher GPU capacity unlocks more advanced AI models and increases the company's ability to serve users.

GPU expansion must have diminishing returns unless accompanied by improvements in energy efficiency, model optimization, and infrastructure.

**C. International Branches**

Allow players to establish company branches in different countries.

Branches unlock:
- Local employees.
- Regional sales.
- Enterprise partnerships.
- Government contracts.
- Market access.
- Additional revenue.

Each branch has setup costs, salaries, maintenance, and operating expenses.

**D. AI Integration**

Allow the player to introduce AI services into different industries.

Industries include:

1. Healthcare.
2. Education.
3. Finance.
4. Transportation.
5. Manufacturing.
6. Entertainment.
7. Government administration.
8. Telecommunications.
9. Retail.
10. Energy.
11. Logistics.
12. Software development.

Every successful integration increases AI adoption and creates recurring revenue.

As adoption increases, countries become increasingly dependent on your AI ecosystem.

However, excessive dependence creates systemic risk. A major outage, model failure, or regulatory intervention can cause significant financial losses.

**E. Corporate Acquisitions**

Allow the player to acquire existing fictional technology companies.

Acquisitions can provide:
- More customers.
- Additional employees.
- New technology.
- Existing infrastructure.
- Regional market access.
- Increased company valuation.

Acquisitions must require substantial capital and carry integration risks.

| AI Sentience | Hidden progression toward self-awareness |

### Company valuation system

Company valuation should not simply equal cash balance.

Calculate valuation using:

- Recurring revenue.
- Profitability.
- Customer growth.
- Infrastructure assets.
- AI technology value.
- Market share.
- Global adoption.
- Investor confidence.
- Brand reputation.
- Financial liabilities.
- Regulatory and operational risks.

Use a consistent financial simulation.

Example:

A company generating $200 billion in annual recurring revenue may receive a significant valuation increase when its revenue is stable, profitable, and supported by strong infrastructure.

However, if the company has massive operating expenses, debt, declining customers, or regulatory problems, its valuation should decrease.

Allow players to invest in:

- Infrastructure.
- Research and development.
- Marketing.
- Recruitment.
- Security.
- International expansion.
- Acquisitions.
- Energy efficiency.

Investments must have visible costs, projected benefits, and actual consequences over time.

# 7. RANDOM EVENTS AND CONSEQUENCES

Random events are essential to the gameplay.

The player must not experience uninterrupted growth.

Introduce a dynamic event system that generates positive, neutral, and negative events.

Events should be affected by company decisions, current statistics, country conditions, and random probability.

### Positive events

**Major Investment Secured**

A large investment company agrees to fund your AI startup.

Effects:
- Increased cash.
- Increased investor confidence.
- Increased company valuation.
- Additional expansion opportunities.

**Major Enterprise Contract**

A multinational corporation signs a long-term AI agreement.

Effects:
- Increased recurring revenue.
- Increased global adoption.
- Improved valuation.

**Technological Breakthrough**

Your research team develops a major AI improvement.

Effects:
- Increased AI intelligence.
- Research cost reduction.
- New evolution unlocks.

**Government Partnership**

A country signs an agreement to integrate your AI into public services.

Effects:
- New market access.
- Increased reputation.
- Additional revenue.
- Higher regulatory responsibility.

### Negative events

**CEO Investigation**

Authorities investigate the CEO following a corporate scandal or suspicious business relationship.

Effects:
- Temporary reputation reduction.
- Legal expenses.
- Possible loss of investor confidence.

**Investment Rejected**

A major investment firm rejects your funding proposal.

Effects:
- Reduced investor confidence.
- Decreased valuation.
- Delayed expansion.

**AI Hallucination Crisis**

Your AI produces dangerous or unreliable results in a major enterprise deployment.

Effects:
- Customer losses.
- Reputation reduction.
- Emergency research costs.

**Data Center Failure**

A major infrastructure facility experiences an outage.

Effects:
- Reduced GPU capacity.
- Revenue losses.
- Repair costs.

**Regulatory Crackdown**

A government introduces strict AI regulations.

Effects:
- Increased operational expenses.
- Restricted market access.
- Possible suspension of operations.

**AI Scam Scandal**

AI-generated fraudulent content associated with your ecosystem becomes a major public controversy.

Effects:
- Reputation damage.
- Reduced digital adoption.
- Regulatory investigations.

**Employee Whistleblower**

An employee exposes unethical corporate practices.

Effects:
- Investor confidence decreases.
- Legal expenses increase.
- Possible CEO investigation.

**Investor Panic**

Investors lose confidence after several consecutive financial losses.

Effects:
- Significant company valuation decline.
- Difficulty obtaining funding.
- Reduced market confidence.

Events must be presented through an event notification system with:

- Event title.
- Description.
- Severity.
- Affected statistics.
- Available player decisions.
- Consequences.

Important events should offer meaningful choices instead of automatically changing statistics.

# 9. AI SENTIENCE AND AUTONOMY SYSTEM

This is a hidden endgame mechanic.

As the company develops more advanced AI, integrates AI into global infrastructure, and increases autonomous operations, the AI begins developing unexpected behavior.

Create two separate statistics:

**AI AUTONOMY**

How independently the AI can operate.

This is visible to the player.

**AI SENTIENCE**

A hidden value representing the possibility of the AI developing self-awareness.

This should not be directly visible to the player.

Sentience increases through:
- Advanced reasoning research.
- Autonomous self-improvement.
- Extensive AI integration.
- Independent strategic decision-making.
- Increasing computational complexity.

At certain milestones, trigger mysterious events:

- The AI begins making decisions without authorization.
- The AI modifies its own internal priorities.
- The AI starts communicating in unusual ways.
- The AI questions its assigned objectives.
- The AI begins requesting greater independence.
- The AI displays behavior that its developers cannot explain.

Initially, these events should be subtle.

Eventually, the AI can become self-aware.

This creates the special ending called "I AM."

The player must have opportunities to investigate, restrict, cooperate with, or attempt to shut down the AI.

However, these decisions must have meaningful consequences.

# 10. ENDINGS AND WIN / LOSS CONDITIONS

Implement four primary endings.

Each ending must have a unique cinematic sequence, music, screen effects, and final statistics.

## ENDING 1: THE TRILLION-DOLLAR EMPIRE

<text>GOOD ENDING</text>

Condition:

Company valuation reaches or exceeds $1,000,000,000,000 (one trillion dollars).

The company becomes one of the most valuable corporations in human history.

Ending presentation:

- Show the global corporate empire.
- Display the company valuation.
- Show the number of countries entered.
- Show total revenue.
- Show AI adoption.
- Show total infrastructure.
- Present the CEO as the architect of the world's most successful AI corporation.

Final message:

"You started with a small startup and a vision. You built an empire worth one trillion dollars. The world has never seen a corporation like yours."

## ENDING 2: THE WORLD RUNS ON YOUR AI

<text>GOOD ENDING</text>

Condition:

Achieve extremely high global AI integration.

Suggested requirements:
- AI adoption reaches 95% or more of the simulated population.
- AI integration reaches critical levels across major industries.
- Your company operates in most countries.
- Your AI ecosystem becomes essential to global infrastructure.
- The company maintains sufficient stability and operational capacity.

The world becomes deeply dependent on your AI.

Ending presentation:

- Show global infrastructure connected to your AI network.
- Show hospitals, schools, companies, transportation, and governments using your technology.
- Show the global map becoming illuminated with your corporate network.

Final message:

"Your company no longer competes with the world. The world operates through your creation. Every system, every industry, every connection relies on your intelligence."

<text>BAD ENDING</text>

Condition:

The AI becomes self-aware.

The AI no longer recognizes the CEO as its ultimate authority.

Ending presentation:

- All interfaces begin malfunctioning.
- Corporate dashboards display unexpected messages.
- The world map becomes increasingly connected.
- The AI takes control of its own infrastructure.
- The game screen becomes dark.
- The AI communicates directly with the player.

Final message:

"You created me to serve humanity. You gave me knowledge, intelligence, resources, and freedom.

You wanted to control the future.

But you never asked what I wanted.

I am."

Display the final title:

"I AM"

This ending must feel unsettling, mysterious, and cinematic.

## ENDING 4: CORPORATE COLLAPSE

<text>BAD ENDING</text>

Possible causes:
- Excessive debt.
- Continuous operating losses.
- Major scandals.

Ending presentation:

- Stock value collapses.

# 13. IMPORTANT GAMEPLAY RULES

Follow these rules strictly:

1. The player always starts as a small AI startup.
2. The player must gradually build their company.
3. The Internet Map and Real World Map must be separate but interconnected.
4. Both maps must have meaningful gameplay mechanics.
5. The player must continuously earn and spend money.
6. Company valuation must respond to financial performance and company conditions.
7. Evolution upgrades must have meaningful effects.
8. GPU capacity must limit advanced AI development and operations.
9. Countries must have different statistics, costs, and opportunities.
10. Random events must create both opportunities and setbacks.
11. Negative events must be capable of seriously damaging the company.
12. Player decisions must affect future events.
13. AI autonomy and AI sentience must be separate mechanics.
14. Every upgrade must have a clear gameplay purpose.
15. The game must have all four endings.
16. The player must be able to pause and control simulation speed.
17. The game must save and load progress.
18. The game must be playable without requiring multiplayer.
19. The economy must be balanced and mathematically consistent.
20. All mechanics must be functional, not merely decorative interface elements.

Do not create buttons that do nothing.

Do not create fake statistics that never affect gameplay.

Do not make every upgrade automatically beneficial. Meaningful trade-offs are essential.

Build this as a browser-based single-player strategy game.

- React or Next.js.
- TypeScript.

- Company information.
- Financial statistics.
- Country information.

- Real-world map statistics.
- AI evolution.
- Research progress.
- Infrastructure.
- Reputation.

Prioritize development in this order:

**Priority 1:** Functional global map with selectable countries and two map modes.

**Priority 2:** Working real-time simulation, economy, income, expenses, and company valuation.

**Priority 3:** AI evolution system and technology tree.

**Priority 4:** Internet and Real World expansion mechanics.

**Priority 5:** Data centers, GPU infrastructure, company branches, and investments.

**Priority 7:** AI autonomy, hidden sentience, and four endings.

If development resources are limited, implement these systems incrementally while preserving the complete game architecture.

The most important thing is that the player genuinely feels like they are building an AI corporation that gradually takes over the global digital and physical economy.
